LEGEND BMW M4 G82

BMW M4 is a high-performance M series sports coupe produced by BMW. The latest generation (G82) is known for its powerful engine, aggressive design, and track performance. Below are the common specs for M4 / M4 Competition.

Engine & Performance

Engine: 3.0L Twin-Turbo inline-6 (S58)

Power

Standard M4 → ~473 hp

M4 Competition → ~503 hp

Competition xDrive → ~523-530 hp

Torque: ~550-650 Nm

0-100 km/h: 3.5 - 4.2 seconds

Top speed: 307 km/h (up to 330 km/h with package)

Transmission: 6-speed manual / 8-speed automatic

Drivetrain: Rear-wheel drive or xDrive AWD

Key Features

M Sport suspension

Adaptive dampers

Launch control

Drift mode (xDrive models)

Carbon fiber roof (optional)

Harman Kardon / Bowers & Wilkins sound system

Digital cluster + iDrive display

M sport seats
